Vault Finance

Category Mobile App UI/UX
Timeline 10 Weeks
Tools Figma, Principle, FigJam
Outcome 4.8★ App Store Rating
UX Research Mobile UI Fintech Prototyping iOS / Android

Overview

Vault Finance is a personal wealth management app designed for young professionals who want to take control of their money without needing a finance degree. My role was to lead the full UX and UI design from initial concept through to development handoff.

The core challenge was making complex financial data feel approachable — even exciting — without sacrificing depth for power users.

The Challenge

The fintech space is crowded, and most apps err on one of two sides: either overwhelming users with information or being so simplified that they lose utility. Vault needed to thread that needle precisely.

Additionally, the onboarding flow had to comply with KYC requirements while feeling as frictionless as a consumer app — a balance most fintech products get badly wrong.

Approach & Process

I conducted 18 user interviews across two rounds of research, synthesised findings into clear personas, and mapped out the core user journeys before any visual design began.

The visual design language was rooted in precision and calm: dark mode first, data visualisations that breathe, and micro-interactions that reward every user action. The onboarding flow was redesigned into a 5-step progressive disclosure that reduced drop-off by 60%.

Outcome

Vault Finance launched with a 4.8-star App Store rating and was featured by Apple as a "New App We Love." Daily active usage exceeded targets by 34% in the first quarter, and the average session length of 6.2 minutes placed it among the top 5% of finance apps globally.
