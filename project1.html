Aura E-Commerce

Category Web Design / UI
Timeline 6 Weeks
Tools Figma, React, Tailwind CSS
Outcome +45% Conversion Rate
Figma UI Design E-Commerce React Conversion Optimisation

Overview

Aura Fashion approached me with a challenge: their existing online store felt dated, difficult to navigate, and was losing customers at every stage of the funnel. They wanted a complete redesign that matched the premium quality of their products.

The project required deep UX research, full visual redesign, and close collaboration with their development team to ensure the final product shipped pixel-perfect.

The Challenge

The existing site had a cluttered navigation, poor mobile experience, and a checkout flow with unnecessary friction — a staggering 72% cart abandonment rate. Users couldn't easily discover products, and the brand aesthetic was inconsistent across pages.

My goal was to create a shopping experience that felt as premium as the clothes themselves — one where every interaction was considered and every page had a clear purpose.

Approach & Process

I started with a two-week discovery phase: heatmap analysis, user interviews, and competitive benchmarking. From there I built a new information architecture and wireframed every key screen before touching the visual design.

The visual direction was inspired by editorial fashion photography — generous whitespace, large imagery, and a monochromatic palette that never competed with the products themselves.

Outcome

Within the first month of launch, conversion rate increased by 45%, average session duration rose by 38%, and cart abandonment fell from 72% to 41%. The client described it as "the best investment in our brand we've ever made."
